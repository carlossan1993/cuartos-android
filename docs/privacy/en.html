Cuartos · Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-24.2 · October 24, 2026

Cuartos lets you design rooms in 2D and 3D. This policy describes the version without accounts, advertising or remote services.

Data that stays on your device

Project and room names and dimensions, doors, windows, colours, furniture, language and preferences are processed and stored locally to provide the design features. Cuartos does not send this data to the developer and includes no analytics, advertising or tracking identifiers.

Files you choose to import, save or share

Only the JSON file you select for import is read. You can export JSON projects and PNG plans. Android lets you select a location or a recipient application. If you select a cloud provider or share a file, that service handles its contents under its own policy. Cuartos does not choose recipients or send projects automatically.

Retention and deletion

Projects remain until you delete them from the project list, clear the application data in Android or uninstall the app. There is no copy on our servers and no account to delete. Exported files and shared copies must be deleted separately from the device, provider or application you selected.

Cache and security

Exports may leave a temporary copy in the app's private cache. A later export attempts to remove export folders older than 24 hours; this is not guaranteed deletion at an exact time. Android may clear the cache, and you can clear it in Android settings. The app uses private storage and temporary grants limited to exported files; it does not request general storage access. It does not provide its own project encryption: protect your device and share only what is necessary.

Permissions and connectivity

The app works offline using bundled resources. It retains Capacitor's standard Internet permission but does not use it to transmit projects or telemetry. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising identifiers.

Contact and external services

If you choose to email csdigital.apps@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the contents you send to address your inquiry. We use that information to reply and retain it while necessary to handle the inquiry; you may request its deletion by writing to the same address. The app does not attach or send projects automatically. Your email provider applies its own policy. If you read this policy on Firebase Hosting, your browser connects to that service, which handles technical connection data under its own policy; this page includes no analytics, tracking cookies or forms.

Changes

If data handling changes, this policy and its date will be updated. The version bundled in the app describes the behaviour of that release.
